# Tonight at Corgi Cafe 🥞🐶

The full stack, live. Tonight, Oct 29, Corgi Cafe, San Francisco.
The plan is the chef's sheet, unchanged: 145 × 9 in pancakes, then 7 in, stop at 45 in, hands off, hold 5 seconds.

## How to watch without toppling it
- Silence zone around the table. No traffic, no bumping, no bass.
- Nothing touches the tower but pancakes. No rings, no rods, no syrup between layers.
- Past ~30 in: no vibration, drafts, or heat lamps within 6 ft.
- Watch for the calls: "44" means slow down, about 10 to go. "STOP" at 45 in means hands off, then the 5-second hold.
- Phones silent, cameras steady. A shaky hand is fine for the stacker — not for the crowd.

## The corgi rule
Low to the ground, high in the sky. Corgis are built low and stable — so is this tower: wide 9 in base, narrow 7 in top, quarter-turn every pancake so tiny thickness differences cancel instead of leaning.

## After the record
Toppings go on after, on the plates. Every pancake is eaten or donated — nothing in the trash. Rejects are eaten, never binned.

## If it falls
That's why there are spares. Re-plan with the measured thickness, cook attempt two, and log it all in TESTLOG.md. Measured numbers beat theory.
