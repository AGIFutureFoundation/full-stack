# Stacking protocol

## Geometry

| Layer | Ring | Pancakes | ≈ Height |
|---|---|---|---|
| Top | 6 in | 50 | 7.6 in |
| Body | 8 in | 200 | 30.4 in |
| Base | 9 in | 50 | 7.6 in |
| **Total** | | **300** | **≈ 45 in** after settling |

Why a pyramid: the bottom layers carry less weight, and every step down gives ~0.5 in of placement tolerance on each side.

Why these sizes: 9 in stays well under the max width even after settling spreads the bottom pancakes; 6 in stays well above the minimum. See [RULES.md](RULES.md).

## Roles

| Role | Job |
|---|---|
| **Stacker** | Places every pancake. Same person at every rehearsal and on the day |
| **Passer** | Hands each pancake on a thin spatula, at stack height, in order |
| **Plumb spotter ×2** | One per laser axis. Calls drift every 10 pancakes |
| **Zone guard** | Silence, no traffic, no robot, nobody touching the table |

## Setup

- Heavy, level table. Check with a digital level, under 0.2°. Matte top.
- A printed 9 in circle on the table to center the first pancake.
- Two cross-line lasers at 90° to each other, projecting vertical plumb lines on the tower. Nothing touches it.
- A freestanding height gauge 2 in from the tower, zero on the table surface, marked at **42.6 / 44 / 45 in**.
- Optional: a horizontal laser line at 45 in. The stacker stops when the top meets the line.
- Tower top at shoulder height for the stacker: no ladder during placement.
- No vibration, drafts, bass speakers or heat lamps within 6 ft once the tower passes ~30 in.

## Placement

1. Hold the pancake by one edge, touch it down, and **lower it like a blanket** to push the air out. Never drop it flat, never slide it.
2. **Rotate each pancake a quarter turn** from the last. Small thickness differences cancel instead of adding up into a lean.
3. Every 10 pancakes, spotters call the drift on their axis. If it drifts, offset the next 5–10 pancakes by 1/16 in the other way.
4. Pace: ~7 s per pancake, ~35 min for the full tower. Don't rush the last third: that's where towers fall.

## Stop rules

- **44 in:** spotter calls "44". Stacker slows down. About 10 top pancakes left.
- **45 in:** "STOP". Stacker steps back, hands off. Stop on height, never on count.
- Hold **5 s** with nobody touching it. Then the official measurement.

## What we never do

- No rings, sleeves or collars around the tower, even "just during the build".
- Nothing inside the tower: no rods, skewers or markers.
- Nothing between layers: no syrup, butter, jam, egg wash.
- No changes to the method on the day that weren't rehearsed.

## Rehearsal checklist

- [ ] Build to **full height** (≥ 45 in) at least once
- [ ] Measure settling over 10 min and log it
- [ ] Measure the 3 bottom pancakes' diameter after 10 min
- [ ] Time the 5 s hold, film everything
- [ ] Test the table, lasers and gauge in the real spot
- [ ] Keep spares for a second attempt
