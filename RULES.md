# Rules

What the record rules say, as publicly reported. The official guidelines go to attempt organizers; the adjudicator on site has the final word.

## As publicly reported

- A pancake is a thin cake made of batter, fried on both sides.
- Each pancake: **diameter 12.5–25 cm (4.9–9.84 in)**, **thickness ≤ 1 cm (0.39 in)**.
- The stack is freestanding: **no internal or external supporting structures**, and no attaching pancakes to each other (no glue, no stringing).
- It must stand **at least 5 seconds** after the last pancake is placed.
- The pancakes must be edible and distributed for consumption, so nothing is wasted.
- Any team size.

Some press reports give the diameter range as **5.5–10 in** instead. We use 7 and 9 in, inside both.

## Current record

**108.3 cm (42.6 in)**, 230 pancakes, set on 19 August 2025 in Sydney, Australia.

## What we're confirming with the adjudicator

1. Exact diameter limits, and when they're measured: before stacking, or after settling?
2. Our full ingredient list, including any texturizer we might switch on.
3. Pre-cooked pancakes brought to room temperature: OK? Any time limit on the build?
4. No-contact guides: lasers, a freestanding height gauge, a printed centering circle on the table.
5. Cooking rings and portion dispensers (cooking equipment only, never in contact with the tower).
6. How height is measured: from which surface, at what moment.
7. How many attempts are allowed on the day.

Answers go here once we have them in writing.
