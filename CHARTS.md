## 4. What the charts are saying

- Height is not linear with pancake count: thinner pancakes
  beat more pancakes. Going from ~6 mm (v1) to ~4.5 mm (v2)
  is what makes 45 in reachable without a wider tower.
- The base carries the tower: 145 of the ~255 pancakes are
  9 in, and they make only ~25.6 in. The top 7 in pancakes
  are lighter, so they can go higher on less base.
- The next records are not about height at all: they are
  about speed, coordination, and scale. That is deliberate —
  height has a ceiling (gravity, settling, a shaky hand),
  but speed, teamwork, and logistics keep scaling.
